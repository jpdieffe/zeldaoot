Connection options

Some networks need a TURN relay. Both players can enter relay details here. Settings stay in this tab.

01 / FIND EACH OTHER

A friend you can see.

Explore as two independent Links. Enter the same area to see your teammate, or use Meet up to catch up.

02 / SHARE THE JOURNEY

Discoveries belong to both.

Share items and quest progress. Each hero has their own view and health, with friendly damage turned off.

03 / TRY TOGETHER

No hero left behind.

If either player dies, both restart at the failed area’s entrance. Your collected items and progress stay with you.

Co-op note: Players, items and quest progress are shared. Fight supported Deku Tree enemies and Gohma together in the same room. Other enemy types and bosses still run separately.

Playing on a phone? Rotate sideways and enable sound after loading. Keep both game tabs visible. The first visit downloads the game engine and assets.
