Shared combat adapts EnemySync and BossAdapters from bghill95/OOT-True-Co-op,
commit 6cd4c47 (https://github.com/bghill95/OOT-True-Co-op), on the project's
pinned zalo/Shipwright engine. Original source comments and engine license
notices are retained. Browser transport, exact uint64 identity encoding,
collision-element requests, authoritative validation, per-collider masks,
targeting, lifecycle and Gohma defeat corrections are project adaptations.

Initial supported roster: Deku Baba, Withered Deku Baba, Skulltula,
Skullwalltula, Deku Scrub, Gohma larvae, Keese, and Queen Gohma.
Other enemies/bosses are intentionally excluded until individually adapted
and verified. Source presence alone is not a browser acceptance-test result.
